## Summary

| | |
|---|---|
| **What it is** | A UK visa for founders whose business an approved endorsing body judges innovative, viable and scalable |
| **Length** | 3 years, extendable for the same business |
| **Settlement** | Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR) after 3 years if the business meets 2 of 7 targets |
| **Fees** | About £10,700 to £11,700 for a single applicant over 3 years (as of Oct 2026), plus the business's own funding |
| **Endorsement rate** | Not published by the Home Office; Envestors reports 34.4% for its own applicants (Apr 2023 to Mar 2026) |
| **Endorsement time** | About 4 to 6 weeks |
| **Visa decision** | Home Office target of 3 weeks from outside the UK, 8 weeks from inside |
| **Rule changes** | Settlement rules are under consultation (see [Rule Changes](#rule-changes)) |

## How It Works

| When | Step |
|---|---|
| Before applying | Write the plan, collect evidence of demand and hold the launch money |
| Endorsement | An endorsing body assesses the plan, then interviews you if it passes |
| Within 3 months of the letter | Apply for the visa, from inside or outside the UK |
| Day of grant | Tell your endorsing body; the 3 years start |
| Months 12 and 24 | Checkpoints with your endorsing body |
| About month 33 | Apply for a settlement endorsement |
| From 28 days before year 3 ends | Apply for ILR, or extend on the same business |

## Requirements

- **Innovative:** customers would switch to it, and a typical competitor could not copy it quickly. The Home Office's guidance for endorsing bodies gives adding an app to a taxi or cleaning company as an example that fails.
- **Viable:** the plan is realistic for your skills and your money, and there is evidence of real demand.
- **Scalable:** there is a structured plan to grow nationally and internationally and to create skilled jobs. A business that stays in one city, or depends on the founder's own hours, is not scalable.

---

### Baseline Rules

- [ ] Aged 18 or over
- [ ] English at level B2 (a UK degree usually satisfies it)
- [ ] £1,270 in personal funds held for 28 days, unless you have been in the UK for 12 months
- [ ] A new business you founded or are founding, not a role in someone else's
- [ ] The business is your main work, and you run it day to day

A skilled side job (RQF level 3 or above) is allowed. To join an existing business, Skilled Worker is the usual route; leaders in their field can look at Global Talent.

## Endorsing Bodies

Only 4 organizations can issue new endorsements (as of Oct 2026). Each runs its own process, as published on its website:

| Body | Assessment | Turnaround |
|---|---|---|
| Innovator International | Plan answered against its published assessment questions; may raise questions on a borderline plan, then interviews plans that pass; 1 free second assessment after a refusal | Up to 40 days |
| Envestors | Its own online business plan form, then a presentation and Q&A call; capital planned for 3 years, with the first 12 months' funding verified and held for 3 months | About 4 to 6 weeks |
| UK Endorsing Services | Point-scored review of the plan, then an interview | 21 working days |
| Global Entrepreneurs Programme | By invitation, for companies based outside the UK with revenue from about £250,000 (depending on sector) and a completed equity raise | Not published |

Another 63 legacy bodies (as of Aug 2026) serve only founders they endorsed before 13 April 2023. Their policies differ, so put anything the rules leave open, such as a side job or outsourced development, to the body you choose in writing before you apply.

## Getting Endorsed

Back every claim in the plan with evidence you can share.

### Founder Skills

You need sector or technical skill, plus the business skill to sell and run the company. Gaps are fine if you name them and budget to fill them.

> [!warning] Own the Technology
> You may subcontract the coding, but you must design the innovation yourself and have someone technical own the build. Home Office guidance gives a sole founder of an AI platform with no technical skill as unlikely to meet the innovation test. Agencies offering to "build it for you" create this risk.

### The Business Plan

There is no official template or length. Write it yourself, using AI for wording at most: the plan has to show your own understanding of the business, and the interview tests it. Keep desk research short and the sales plan specific, and show the product with screenshots or a demo.

Write forecasts you can beat: cost every item, get supplier quotes for anything costly, model 3 scenarios, and divide annual sales by 365 to check the daily figure is believable. Forecasts become the benchmark at every checkpoint and at settlement, so state any pre-revenue or loss-making years up front.

### Evidence of Demand

Desk research alone is weak. Instead:

1. Interview likely customers about their problem.
2. Test a priced solution with them.
3. Collect letters of intent from the people who hold the budget, sent from company email addresses.

Name contacts in the appendix so the letters can be checked.

### Funding

There is no fixed minimum. You need enough to cover the deepest dip in your cash-flow forecast, plus contingency. The money must be cash you already hold, shown on bank statements; property, future salary and a raise planned after arrival do not count. Money from someone else needs their bank evidence and a signed letter of terms. Build the main forecast on money you hold and show any raise as a second scenario.

---

### Before You Submit

- [ ] Plan written by you, with the product shown
- [ ] Supplier quotes for every costly item
- [ ] Forecast in 3 scenarios, built on money you hold
- [ ] Pre-revenue or loss-making years stated
- [ ] Letters of intent from budget holders, with contacts named in the appendix
- [ ] Bank statements for the launch money, and funder evidence for any third-party money
- [ ] Innovation designed by you, with a technical owner for the build
- [ ] Evidence ready for every claim in the plan

---

### Interview and Due Diligence

The interview tests that you wrote the plan and can explain your numbers. Due diligence covers your identity, sanctions checks and the source of your funds. Deal with the bodies directly: scammers forge endorsement letters and falsely claim to partner with a body.

### After a Refusal

Innovator International offers 1 free second assessment and Envestors has an appeals process; use them when you can point to facts the assessor missed. A substantially rewritten proposal is usually a new paid application, and a refusal by one body does not bind another.

## Applying for the Visa

Request the endorsement letter only when the visa application is ready: it is valid for 3 months. Upload the plan and evidence again with the application, because the caseworker reads the file afresh.

### Switching From Another Visa

Most visa holders, Graduate, Student and Skilled Worker included, can switch inside the UK. Visitors cannot and must apply from outside.

- **Graduate:** a Graduate visa applied for from 1 January 2027 lasts 18 months, not 2 years (PhD graduates keep 3 years), so start the plan early.
- **Student:** you can switch once you complete your course, or during a PhD after at least 24 months of study (the Rules say 24; the caseworker guidance says 12). Since 25 November 2025 you may work self-employed on the business while your visa application is pending.

If you apply before your current visa expires, your permission to stay (called leave) continues while the Home Office decides. Apply well before it expires, leaving time for a second endorsement attempt. The 3 years start when the Innovator Founder visa is granted; time on other visas does not count.

### Processing Times

The Home Office aims to decide in 3 weeks from outside the UK and 8 weeks from inside. Paid priority takes 5 working days and super priority the next working day.

> [!warning] Rising Refusals After Endorsement
> Endorsement does not guarantee the visa. Refusals of in-UK applications by main applicants (switches and extensions on this route and the old Innovator route) rose from 3.2% in 2021 (5 of 154) to 31.4% in Q2 2026 (32 of 102). Keep evidence you can share for every claim in the plan.

## Running the Business

### First Steps

- [ ] Endorsing body told the day the visa is granted (it may not hear from the Home Office, and checkpoints count from that date)
- [ ] Company registered (£100 online), with you as a director and your Companies House identity verified
- [ ] Business bank account open (often the slowest step)
- [ ] All business money running through that account (settlement evidence is read from its statements)
- [ ] Main trading premises in place by month 24 (a co-working space or incubator counts)

---

### Checkpoints

Your endorsing body meets you at 12 and 24 months and visits your main trading premises at one of them. Attend both: a body can withdraw endorsement from a founder who stops keeping in contact, and an extension needs at least 2 checkpoint meetings.

- **12 months:** progress should be reasonable for the time; profit is not expected.
- **24 months:** the body also expects commercial progress, such as engaged customers or sales.

### Changing the Business Plan

Agree any change with your endorsing body before you make it. A substantially different business is assessed afresh, usually for a fee. If a forecast proves wrong, ask the body to agree a revised one rather than carry a target you cannot meet.

### When Endorsement Is Withdrawn

Your endorsing body can withdraw the endorsement if the business never starts, you never move to the UK, it doubts you are genuine, or you stop keeping in contact. The Home Office then normally cancels your permission with 60 days left. If the body is removed from the list or gives up its status, you normally get 90 days. Either way, you should be allowed to find another body.

## Settlement (ILR) After 3 Years

Apply for a settlement endorsement about 3 months before your visa ends: the body takes 4 to 6 weeks, and your ILR application must be in before your leave expires. The body must confirm that the business is active, trading and sustainable for the next 12 months, has made significant achievements against the endorsed plan and is run by you day to day. It must also meet at least 2 of these 7 targets:

- £50,000 invested and actively spent on the business
- Customers at least doubled in 3 years and above the average for similar UK businesses (no reliable benchmark exists)
- Significant R&D plus a UK intellectual property application (a trademark does not count)
- £1 million annual gross revenue
- £500,000 revenue, £100,000 of it from exports
- 10 full-time jobs for settled workers
- 5 full-time jobs for settled workers, each paying at least £25,000 a year

Meeting both jobs targets counts as 1 target, not 2. Evidence a third target where you can, in case one is disallowed.

### Meeting the Targets

- **£50,000:** new money you put in, not reinvested profit. Both the money in and the spending must show on the company's business account. Visa and endorsement fees and your own salary do not count.
- **Jobs:** each must last 12 months at an average of 30 hours a week, so to settle at 3 years, hire by about month 22. Put everyone you count, a co-founder included, on a written employment contract.
- **Pay:** the rules require 5 jobs "each of which has a mean salary of at least £25,000", so each role must reach it on its own.
- **Settled workers:** British or Irish citizens and people with ILR or EU settled or pre-settled status. Graduate, Student and Skilled Worker visa holders do not count.
- **Co-founders:** you cannot share a target. If both of you rely on the 5 jobs, the business needs 10.
- **Cost of the 5-jobs target:** about £132,000 to £143,000 a year including employer National Insurance and pension (2026 to 2027 rates), on top of the £50,000.

---

### The Sustainability Test

The rule asks whether the business is "sustainable for at least the following 12 months, based on its assets and expected income, weighed against its current and planned expenses." It looks forward, so evidence it with accounts, bank statements and a 12-month forecast for the endorsed business itself.

### The Extension Fallback

If progress is significant but short of the targets, the body can offer a same-business endorsement instead, on the same timing as settlement. The extension lasts 3 years, with checkpoints again; extensions can repeat, and you can apply for ILR once the targets are met.

---

### Before Applying for ILR

- [ ] No more than 180 days outside the UK in any 12 months
- [ ] Life in the UK test passed (if you are 18 to 64)
- [ ] Settlement endorsement in hand
- [ ] Application submitted no earlier than 28 days before you complete 3 years, and before your leave expires
- [ ] No travel outside the UK, Ireland, the Channel Islands or the Isle of Man until the decision (up to 6 months)

## Family

Your partner and children under 18 can apply as your dependants. Each pays the visa fee and the Immigration Health Surcharge (£1,035 a year for adults, £776 for children). Unless you have all been in the UK for 12 months, each also needs maintenance funds held for 28 days: £285 for a partner, £315 for the first child and £200 for each further child.

- **Partner:** married, in a civil partnership, or together for at least 2 years (living together, or able to show ongoing commitment). A partner can work in any job except as a professional sportsperson or sports coach. They need 5 continuous years in the UK as your partner before settling, so one who arrives with you settles about 2 years after you, and they must pass the Life in the UK test.
- **Children:** can apply to settle with you.

Each dependant pays the ILR fee (£3,226) when they settle.

From 26 March 2027, partners and children need B2 English speaking and listening to settle, up from B1. You already meet B2 as a founder.

## Costs

Fees to settlement for a single applicant applying inside the UK (as of Oct 2026).

| Item | Cost |
|---|---:|
| Initial endorsement | £1,000 |
| Visa application, inside the UK | £1,693 |
| Immigration Health Surcharge, 3 years at £1,035 | £3,105 |
| Checkpoints at 12 and 24 months, £500 each | £1,000 |
| Settlement endorsement | £1,000 |
| ILR application plus Life in the UK test | £3,276 |
| VAT at 20% on endorsing-body fees, where charged (UK residents) | £600 |
| **Total** | **£11,674** |

- **From outside the UK:** the visa fee is £1,357, £336 less.
- **Same-business extension:** about £7,200 more: the £1,693 visa fee, £3,105 health surcharge and about £2,400 in endorsing-body fees with VAT.
- **Refused endorsement:** fees are not refunded.

## Rule Changes

### Earned Settlement

A Home Office consultation (Nov 2025 to Feb 2026) proposed raising the standard wait for settlement from 5 to 10 years. It says "most individuals" on the Innovator Founder route "should continue to benefit from an accelerated route to settlement after 3 years, subject to the mandatory requirements", and its proposal to require earnings above £12,570 a year for 3 to 5 years names no exemption for them. The changes would apply to everyone who does not yet have ILR. No government response had been published as of Oct 2026.

### The Endorsing-Body Contract

The Home Office contract for endorsing-body services runs to 23 October 2028, and the published notice allows an extension. The Migration Advisory Committee is reviewing the talent routes, the endorsing-body model included, and intends to finish in winter 2026.

## FAQ

### Do I Need £50,000 to Get Endorsed?

No. The fixed £50,000 was removed in 2023; it survives only as one settlement target.

### Do I Need a Company or Bank Account Before Applying?

No. A company, trading and a business bank account are only needed once you are on the visa, at the checkpoints, extension and settlement.

### Can I Apply Without a Co-Founder?

Yes, sole founders qualify. Co-founders each make their own application and pay their own fees on the same plan, and each needs their own 2 settlement targets.

### Is There a Minimum Shareholding?

No. You must be a director or member of the company and run it day to day, not act only as an investor.

### Can I Pay Myself a Salary?

Yes, but your salary does not count toward the £50,000 target.

### What Happens if My Business Fails?

Your endorsement can be [withdrawn](#when-endorsement-is-withdrawn), leaving you about 60 days to find another body or leave. A business that is behind but still making significant progress can [extend](#the-extension-fallback) instead of settling.

### Can I Spend Time Outside the UK?

Yes. The visa sets no absence limit, but you must move to the UK and run the business here, and ILR allows no more than 180 days outside the UK in any 12 months.

### Can I Claim Benefits?

No. The visa gives no access to public funds, which covers most benefits and housing help.

### When Can I Become a British Citizen?

Usually 12 months after ILR (straight away if you are married to a British citizen), once you have 5 years in the UK with no more than 450 days outside it, and no more than 90 in the last 12 months. The fee is £1,839 (as of Oct 2026).
