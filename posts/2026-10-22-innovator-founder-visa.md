# The Innovator Founder Visa Guide

The UK Innovator Founder visa lets you run your own business in the UK and can lead to settlement after 3 years, but about two in three applications fail at endorsement. This guide covers what decides each step.

## Summary

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| **What it is** | A UK visa for founders whose business an approved endorsing body has judged innovative, viable and scalable |
| **Who it's for** | Founders with an original idea, the skills to run it, and the money to launch it |
| **Length** | 3 years, extendable for the same business |
| **Path to ILR** | Indefinite Leave to Remain after 3 years if the business meets two of seven targets |
| **Total cost** | About £10,700 to £11,700 in fees for one person over 3 years (as of Oct 2026), plus the business's own capital |
| **Endorsement odds** | About a third of applications are endorsed (Envestors published 34.4% for Apr 2023 to Mar 2026) |
| **Decision times** | Endorsement: about 4 to 6 weeks. Visa: 3 to 8 weeks on paper, often 3 to 6 months in practice |

## How It Works

1. **Build the case.** Write a business plan, gather evidence that customers want the product, and hold the money to launch it. No company, revenue or trading history is required.
2. **Get endorsed.** Apply to an endorsing body, which assesses the plan and, if it passes, interviews you. Success produces an endorsement letter.
3. **Apply for the visa.** Use the letter within 3 months to apply to the Home Office, from inside or outside the UK.
4. **Run the business.** The endorsing body checks progress at 12 and 24 months after the visa is granted and visits your premises once.
5. **Settle.** After 3 years, a settlement endorsement plus two of seven growth targets leads to Indefinite Leave to Remain. If you are not ready, a same-business extension buys more time.

## Requirements

The business must pass three tests:

- **Innovative:** it offers something customers would switch to, and an ordinary competitor in the sector could not copy it quickly. Adding an app to a taxi or cleaning company is the Home Office's own example of what fails.
- **Viable:** the plan is realistic for your skills and your money, and there is evidence of real demand.
- **Scalable:** there is a structured plan to grow nationally and internationally and to create skilled jobs. A business that stays in one city, or depends on the founder's own hours, is not scalable. The innovation and its control must sit in the UK.

---

### Baseline Rules

- [ ] Aged 18 or over
- [ ] English at level B2 (a UK degree usually satisfies it)
- [ ] Personal maintenance funds of £1,270 held for 28 days, unless you have been in the UK for 12 months
- [ ] A new business: you founded it or are founding it, rather than joining one that already trades (your own company trading before endorsement is fine)
- [ ] You run it day to day; a skilled side job (RQF level 3 or above) is allowed if the business stays your main work
- [ ] A sector your endorsing body assesses (crypto, adult content, politically or religiously driven projects and simple aggregators are commonly refused)

## Endorsing Bodies

Only four organizations can issue new endorsements (as of Oct 2026). The three private bodies charge the same Home Office set fee of £1,000 per assessment, plus VAT if you live in the UK.

| Body | Best For | Turnaround | Published Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Innovator International** | Most sectors | About 4 weeks, usually up to 40 days | Not published |
| **Envestors** | Investment-ready founders with long runway | About 4 to 6 weeks | 34.4% (Apr 2023 to Mar 2026) |
| **UK Endorsing Services** | R&D-led and deep-tech businesses | 21 working days | Not published |
| **Global Entrepreneurs Programme** | Established overseas companies, by invitation only | Not published | Not published |

Each body also has its own requirements:

- **Innovator International**
  - Publishes its assessment questions; answer each one inside your plan
  - One free second assessment after a refusal
- **Envestors**
  - Its own online business plan form
  - Wants capital planned for the first 3 years, with the first 12 months' funding verified and held for 3 months
- **UK Endorsing Services**
  - A point-scored review of the plan, then an interview
- **Global Entrepreneurs Programme**
  - Applicant must be based outside the UK, with revenue from about £250,000 (depending on sector) and a completed equity raise

63 legacy bodies (as of Aug 2026) can still act only for founders they endorsed before 13 April 2023. If a body loses its status, the Home Office normally cuts its founders' leave to 90 days so they can find another body.

> [!tip] Why the Choice Matters
> The bodies read one rulebook differently on side jobs, outsourcing, combining existing products and funding evidence. A refusal by one does not bind another, but every attempt costs a fee and months, so compare the bodies before applying. Deal with them directly: forged endorsement letters and firms falsely claiming to partner with a body are in circulation.

## Getting Endorsed

### What Assessors Check

Assessors usually read your CV first, then the plan, market evidence and finances. They will not research claims for you, so an unevidenced claim counts as missing.

### The Business Plan

Aim for about 30 pages plus appendices. There is no official template, and factory-made plans draw suspicion. Assessors look for the founder's own thinking; polishing the wording with AI is very different from generating the plan with it. Keep desk research short and the sales plan specific, and show the product with screenshots or a demo.

Write forecasts you can beat. They become the benchmark at every checkpoint and at settlement, so state any pre-revenue or loss-making years up front.

### Evidence of Demand

Most applicants bring only desk research. Interview about ten likely customers about their problem, then test a priced solution, then collect letters of intent from people who hold the budget. Five to ten letters from company addresses beat a survey of thousands. Name contacts in the appendix: assessors may phone them.

### Funding

Your funding requirement is the lowest point in your cash-flow forecast plus contingency, with at least 12 months of runway; there is no fixed minimum. The money must be liquid and held for some months (bodies ask for 3 to 6 months of statements). Property, a car for sale and future salary do not count. Third-party money needs the funder's bank evidence and a signed letter of terms.

> [!tip] Key Rule
> Unsecured money does not count. A pending grant, an investor "in talks" or a raise planned after arrival is upside. Build one forecast on the money you hold and show the raise as a second scenario.

### Founder Skills

You need sector or technical skill, plus the business skill to sell and run the company. Gaps are fine if named and funded. A founder with only a small minority stake can expect questions about control.

> [!warning] Common Mistake
> You may subcontract the coding, but you must design the innovation yourself and have someone technical own the build. The Home Office's guidance for endorsing bodies cites, as a case to refuse, a sole founder of an AI platform with no technical expertise to take ownership of it. Consultancies that offer to "build it for you" lead applicants straight into this.

---

### Top Refusal Reasons

| Reason | Fix |
|---|---|
| **Weak innovation** | Show why customers would pay the cost of switching from what they use now, and what makes it hard to copy |
| **Weak development plan** | Name who builds what, by when, with supplier quotes for anything costly |
| **Unrealistic forecasts** | Break annual sales into a daily figure, model three scenarios, cost every item |
| **No customer validation** | Replace market statistics with buyer interviews and letters of intent |

Send your strongest version first: later applications are read alongside earlier ones. After a refusal, a second assessment helps only when you can point to facts the assessor missed, and the window to ask is short. A rewritten proposal is a new paid application, at any body.

---

### Interview and Due Diligence

Bodies differ in order. Innovator International contacts borderline files first and interviews only plans that pass; Envestors interviews as part of every assessment. The interview checks you wrote the plan and can explain your numbers. Due diligence covers identity, sanctions and the source of funds.

## Applying and Processing Times

The endorsement letter is valid for 3 months, so do not request it too early. Apply from outside the UK, or switch from most visas inside it. Expect to upload the plan and evidence again, because the caseworker reads the file afresh.

Published standards are 3 weeks from outside the UK and 8 weeks from inside, with paid priority (5 working days) and super priority (next working day). Founders report 3 to 6 months in practice as of Oct 2026, priority included, and priority slots are hard to book. Plan leases and notice periods around the longer figure.

> [!warning] Rising Refusals After Endorsement
> The in-country refusal rate for main applicants (switches and extensions, Innovator and Innovator Founder routes) rose from 3.2% in 2021 to 31.4% in Q2 2026. Caseworkers send written questions about who built the product, funding, pricing and claimed customer conversations. Keep evidence you can share for every claim in the plan.

## Costs

Lifetime fees for one applicant applying inside the UK, as of Oct 2026. Endorsing-body fees carry 20% VAT if you live in the UK.

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Initial endorsement | £1,000 (£1,200 with VAT) |
| Visa application, inside the UK | £1,693 |
| Immigration Health Surcharge, 3 years at £1,035 | £3,105 |
| 12- and 24-month checkpoints | £1,000 (£1,200 with VAT) |
| Settlement endorsement | £1,000 (£1,200 with VAT) |
| ILR application plus Life in the UK test | £3,276 |
| **Total** | **£11,074 (£11,674 with VAT)** |

Applying from outside the UK, the visa fee is £1,357, so the total is £10,738 (£11,338 with VAT). Add about £7,198 for a 3-year same-business extension if the business is not ready to settle. Each refused endorsement costs £1,000 (plus VAT), non-refundable. Family members add their own fees; see [Family](#family).

---

### Capital the Business Needs

Beyond its launch funding, the business has to pay for the settlement targets: the £50,000 investment, and five jobs at £25,000, which cost about £132,000 to £143,000 a year with employer National Insurance and pension (2026 to 2027 rates).

## After You Arrive

### First Steps

Register the company (£100 online) and complete Companies House identity verification. Open a business bank account early; it is the slowest step. Run all business money through it from day one, because settlement evidence is read from its statements. Without UK credit history, landlords often want 3 to 6 months' rent upfront.

---

### 12- and 24-Month Checkpoints

Both count from the visa grant. At 12 months, progress should match the time put in; profit is not expected. At 24 months the bar rises to progress on the innovation plus commercial progress, such as engaged customers or sales. At one checkpoint the body visits your main trading premises. A co-working space or incubator counts. No premises by 24 months puts the endorsement at risk.

---

### Changing the Business Plan

Agree any change with your endorsing body before making it. A new direction must itself be innovative, viable and scalable, and a substantially different business is assessed afresh, usually for a fee. If a forecast proves wrong, ask the body to re-baseline it rather than carry a target you cannot meet. Revenue from activity the body never approved may not count at settlement.

---

### When Endorsement Is Withdrawn

Bodies withdraw when the business never started, the founder never moved or is not genuine, or checkpoint meetings are missed. The Home Office then normally cuts leave to 60 days and should let you find another body. You can still apply again later.

## Settlement (ILR) After 3 Years

After 3 years on this route, you apply for a settlement endorsement, about 3 months before your visa ends: the body's review takes 4 to 6 weeks, and the Home Office application must be in before your leave expires. The body must confirm that the business is active, trading and sustainable for 12 months, with significant achievements against the endorsed plan, your day-to-day role, and at least two of these seven targets:

| Criterion | Notes |
|---|---|
| £50,000 invested and actively spent on the business | The most common choice |
| Customers at least doubled in 3 years, and above the average for similar UK businesses | No reliable benchmark exists |
| Significant R&D plus a UK intellectual property application | A trademark does not count |
| £1 million annual gross revenue | |
| £500,000 revenue, £100,000 of it from exports | |
| 10 full-time jobs for settled workers | |
| 5 full-time jobs for settled workers, each with a mean salary of at least £25,000 | Usually paired with the £50,000 |

The guidance for endorsing bodies treats the two jobs targets as one, so they cannot make up your pair. The pair most founders aim for is the £50,000 plus the five jobs.

- **The £50,000** must be new money you put in, not reinvested profit. Both the money in and the spending must show on the company's business account. Visa and endorsement fees and your own salary do not count.
- **The jobs** must each last 12 months at an average of 30 hours a week, so hires for settlement at year 3 must start by about month 22. Each role needs at least £25,000 on its own, not on average. Workers must be settled: British or Irish citizens, people with EU settled or pre-settled status, other ILR holders and UK Ancestry visa holders, but not Graduate, Student or Skilled Worker visa holders. Anyone counted, a co-founder included, needs a written employment contract.
- **Co-founders** cannot share a target: if both rely on the five-jobs target, the business needs ten jobs.

Before paying, check your own eligibility: no more than 180 days outside the UK in any 12 months, and the Life in the UK test if you are 18 to 64. ILR decisions can take up to 6 months.

---

### The Sustainability Test

The rule asks whether the business is "sustainable for at least the following 12 months, based on its assets and expected income, weighed against its current and planned expenses." In practice, bodies look for sales covering costs, including a market-rate salary for the founder role. Cash already in the business counts as an asset, but savings covering a trading loss will not normally pass. Revenue counts only if it comes from the endorsed innovation through the endorsed company: income from doing the same job by hand, from consultancy that has become the main business, or banked through another company may be excluded.

---

### The Extension Fallback

If progress is significant but short of the targets, the body can offer a same-business endorsement instead, on the same 3-month timing. Extensions can repeat, and you can apply for ILR whenever ready. Settling at exactly 3 years is not expected; most founders move on to at least one extension.

## Switching From Another Visa

- **Graduate visa:** you can switch inside the UK. Graduate visas applied for from 1 January 2027 last 18 months, not 2 years (PhD graduates keep 3 years), so start the plan early.
- **Student visa:** you can switch inside the UK once you complete your course, or during a PhD after at least 24 months of study. Since 25 November 2025 (HC 1333), you may be self-employed on the business while an endorsed application made on Student leave is pending.
- **Skilled Worker:** you can switch inside the UK.
- **Visitor:** you cannot switch; apply from outside the UK.

Apply with time to spare. An application made before your visa expires keeps your leave going while it is decided, but if it is refused after the expiry date you cannot make a fresh application from inside the UK. Leave room for a second endorsement attempt.

Time on any other visa does not count toward the 3 years. The clock starts when the Innovator Founder visa is granted.

## Family

Your partner and children under 18 can apply as your dependants. Each pays the visa fee and the Immigration Health Surcharge (£1,035 a year for adults, £776 for children). Unless you have all been in the UK for 12 months, they also need maintenance funds held for 28 days: £285 for a partner, £315 for the first child and £200 for each further child.

A partner can work in almost any job. Children can settle when you do. A partner needs 5 continuous years in the UK as your partner before settling, so a partner who arrives with you settles about 2 years after you. Your partner must meet the English and Life in the UK requirements.

## Risks and Rule Changes

- **Earned settlement.** A consultation (Nov 2025 to Feb 2026) proposes raising the standard wait for settlement from 5 to 10 years. Innovator Founders would keep an accelerated 3-year path, but the paper also proposes earnings above £12,570 a year for 3 to 5 years and asks whether the changes should apply to people already on a route. As of Oct 2026, no government response has been published.
- **B2 English for family settlement.** From 26 March 2027 (HC 1691), partners and children need B2 speaking and listening to settle, up from B1. Founders themselves proved B2 on entry and face no new test.
- **Endorsing bodies.** The Home Office contract for endorsing-body services runs to 23 October 2028, and the Migration Advisory Committee is reviewing the endorsing-body model, with a report due in winter 2026. If a body loses its status, the 90-day rule above applies.
- **Falling grants.** Grants to main applicants (inside and outside the UK) fell from 880 in 2023 to 269 in the first half of 2026, a pace of about 540 a year.

## FAQ

### Can I Work for Another Company on the Innovator Founder Visa?

Yes, in a skilled job (RQF level 3 or above) if the business stays your main activity, though bodies differ in how much outside work they accept.

### Do I Need £50,000 to Get Endorsed?

No. The fixed £50,000 was removed in 2023; it survives only as one settlement target.

### Can I Apply Before Starting a Company or Trading?

Yes. Idea-stage businesses can be endorsed, and so can an existing business you founded.

### Can Two Co-Founders Apply Together?

Yes, each with a separate application and fees on the same plan. Each needs their own two settlement targets, and the business must support both.

### Does Time on a Graduate Visa Count Toward ILR?

No. Only time on the Innovator Founder visa counts toward its 3-year settlement period.
