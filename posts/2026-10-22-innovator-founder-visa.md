# The Innovator Founder Visa Guide

This guide explains how the UK Innovator Founder visa works, from getting endorsed to settling after three years.

## Summary

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| **What it is** | A UK visa for founders whose business an approved endorsing body has judged innovative, viable and scalable |
| **Who it's for** | Founders with an original idea, the skills to run it, and the money to launch it |
| **Length** | 3 years, extendable for the same business |
| **Path to ILR** | Indefinite Leave to Remain after 3 years if the business meets two of seven targets |
| **Total cost** | About £11,100 to £11,700 in fees for one person over 3 years (as of Oct 2026), plus the business's own capital |
| **Endorsement odds** | About a third of applications are endorsed (Envestors published 34.4% for Apr 2023 to Mar 2026) |
| **Decision times** | Endorsement: about 4 to 6 weeks. Visa: 3 to 8 weeks on paper, often 3 to 6 months in practice |

## How It Works

1. **Build the case.** Write a business plan, gather evidence that customers want the product, and hold the money to launch it. No company, revenue or trading history is required.
2. **Get endorsed.** Apply to an endorsing body, which assesses the plan and, if it passes, interviews you. Success produces an endorsement letter.
3. **Apply for the visa.** Use the letter within 3 months to apply to the Home Office, from inside or outside the UK.
4. **Run the business.** The endorsing body checks progress at 12 and 24 months after the visa is granted and visits your premises once.
5. **Settle.** After 3 years, a settlement endorsement plus two of seven growth targets leads to Indefinite Leave to Remain. If you are not ready, a same-business extension buys more time.

## Requirements: Who Qualifies

The business must pass three tests:

- **Innovative:** it offers something customers would switch to, and an ordinary competitor in the sector could not copy it quickly. Adding an app to a taxi or cleaning company is the Home Office's own example of what fails.
- **Viable:** the plan is realistic for your skills and your money, and there is evidence of real demand.
- **Scalable:** there is a structured plan to grow nationally and internationally and to create skilled jobs. A business that stays in one city, or depends on the founder's own hours, is not scalable.

---

### Baseline Rules

- [ ] Aged 18 or over
- [ ] English at level B2 (a UK degree usually satisfies it)
- [ ] Personal maintenance funds of £1,270 held for 28 days, unless you have been in the UK for 12 months
- [ ] A new business: you founded it or are founding it, rather than joining one that already trades (your own company trading before endorsement is fine)
- [ ] You run it day to day; a skilled side job (RQF level 3 or above) is allowed if the business stays your main work
- [ ] Not working as a professional sportsperson or sports coach
- [ ] Not a sector your endorsing body will not assess (crypto, adult content, politically or religiously driven projects and simple aggregators are commonly refused)

## Endorsing Bodies

Only four organizations can issue new endorsements (as of Oct 2026). The three private bodies charge the same Home Office set fee of £1,000 per assessment, plus VAT if you live in the UK.

| Body | Best For | Turnaround | Published Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Innovator International** | Most sectors | About 4 weeks, usually up to 40 days | Not published |
| **Envestors** | Investment-ready founders with long runway | About 4 to 6 weeks | 34.4% (Apr 2023 to Mar 2026) |
| **UK Endorsing Services** | R&D-led and deep-tech businesses | 21 working days | Not published |
| **Global Entrepreneurs Programme** | Established overseas companies | By invitation | Not published |

Each body also has its own requirements:

- **Innovator International**
  - Publishes the questions its assessors answer
  - One free second assessment after a refusal
- **Envestors**
  - Its own online business plan form
  - Wants capital planned for the first 3 years, with the first 12 months' funding verified and held for 3 months
- **UK Endorsing Services**
  - A point-scored review of the plan, then an interview
  - Payment is due before work starts
- **Global Entrepreneurs Programme**
  - Applicant must be based outside the UK, with revenue from about £250,000 (depending on sector) and a completed equity raise

63 legacy bodies (as of Aug 2026) can still act only for founders they endorsed before 13 April 2023. If a body loses its status, the Home Office normally cuts its founders' leave to 90 days so they can find another body.

> [!tip] Why the Choice Matters
> The bodies read one rulebook differently on side jobs, outsourcing, combining existing products and funding evidence. A refusal by one does not bind another, but every attempt costs a fee and months, so it pays to compare the bodies before applying.

## Getting Endorsed

### What Assessors Check

Assessors usually read your CV first, then the plan, market evidence and finances. The question is whether this person can deliver this business. The plan must show, with evidence, that the project has a strong chance of success. Assessors will not research claims for you, so an unevidenced claim counts as missing.

### The Business Plan

Aim for about 30 pages plus appendices. There is no official template, and factory-made plans draw suspicion. Assessors look for the founder's own thinking; polishing the wording with AI is very different from generating the plan with it. Keep desk research short and the sales plan specific, and show the product with screenshots or a demo.

### Evidence of Demand

Most applicants bring only desk research, which is the weakest kind of evidence. Interview about ten likely customers about their problem, then test a priced solution, then collect letters of intent from people who hold the budget. Five to ten letters from company addresses beat a survey of thousands. Name contacts in the appendix: assessors may phone them.

### Funding

Your funding requirement is the lowest point in your cash-flow forecast plus contingency, with at least 12 months of runway. There is no fixed minimum; the figure is whatever your forecast shows the business needs. The money must be liquid and held for months, shown on bank statements. Property, a car for sale and future salary do not count. Third-party money needs the funder's bank evidence and a signed letter of terms.

> [!tip] Key Rule
> Unsecured money does not count. A pending grant, an investor "in talks" or a raise planned after arrival is upside, not funding. Build one forecast on the money you hold and show the raise as a second scenario.

### Founder Skills

You need sector or technical skill, plus the business skill to sell and run the company. Gaps are fine if named and funded. You may subcontract the coding, but you must design the innovation yourself, and someone technical must oversee the build. A founder with only a small minority stake can expect questions about control.

> [!warning] Common Mistake
> The Home Office's guidance for endorsing bodies gives the example of a sole founder of an AI platform with no technical expertise to take ownership of the build. Outsourcing the innovation itself is a separate reason to refuse. Consultancies that offer to "build it for you" lead applicants straight into both.

---

### Top Refusal Reasons

| Reason | Fix |
|---|---|
| **Weak innovation** | Show why customers would pay the cost of switching from what they use now, and what makes it hard to copy |
| **No customer validation** | Replace market statistics with buyer interviews and letters of intent |
| **Weak development plan** | Name who builds what, by when, with supplier quotes for anything costly |
| **Unrealistic forecasts** | Break annual sales into a daily figure, model three scenarios, cost every item |
| **Viability or scalability** | Show phased growth beyond one city and how most income comes from the innovation |
| **Founder capability** | Tie your CV to the plan honestly and fund every skills gap |

After a refusal, Innovator International offers one free second assessment. It helps only when you can point to a fact the assessor missed; a rewritten proposal is a new paid application.

---

### Interview and Due Diligence

Borderline files get a call or email first; clear refusals can arrive within days with no contact. A plan that passes leads to an online interview that checks you wrote the plan and can explain your numbers. Due diligence covers identity, sanctions and the source of funds.

## Applying and Processing Times

The endorsement letter is valid for 3 months, so do not request it too early. Apply from outside the UK, or switch from most visas inside it. Expect to upload the plan and evidence again, because the caseworker reads the file afresh.

Published standards are 3 weeks from outside the UK and 8 weeks from inside, with paid priority (5 working days) and super priority (next working day). Founders report 3 to 6 months in practice as of Oct 2026, priority included, and priority slots are hard to book. Leases and notice periods are safer planned around the longer figure.

> [!warning] Rising Refusals After Endorsement
> The in-country refusal rate for main applicants on this route (switches and extensions) rose from 3.2% in 2021 to 31.4% in Q2 2026. Caseworkers send written questions about who built the product, funding, pricing and claimed customer conversations. Keep evidence you can share for every claim in the plan.

## Costs

Lifetime fees for one applicant applying inside the UK, as of Oct 2026. Endorsing-body fees carry 20% VAT if you live in the UK.

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Initial endorsement | £1,000 (£1,200 with VAT) |
| Visa application, inside the UK | £1,693 |
| Immigration Health Surcharge, 3 years at £1,035 | £3,105 |
| 12- and 24-month checkpoints | £1,000 (£1,200 with VAT) |
| Settlement endorsement | £1,000 (£1,200 with VAT) |
| ILR application plus Life in the UK test | £3,276 |
| **Total** | **£11,074 (£11,674 with VAT)** |

Applying from outside the UK, the visa fee is £1,357, so the total is £10,738 (£11,338 with VAT). Add about £7,198 for a 3-year same-business extension if the business is not ready to settle. Each refused endorsement costs £1,000 (plus VAT), non-refundable. Family members add their own fees; see [Family](#family).

---

### Capital the Business Needs

The business also needs its own funding, sized by its forecast. Settlement targets add more: £50,000 actively spent, or about £143,000 a year for five jobs at £25,000 with employer National Insurance and pension (about £132,000 if the business can claim the £10,500 Employment Allowance; 2026 to 2027 rates). Without UK credit history, landlords often want 3 to 6 months' rent upfront.

## After You Arrive

### First Steps

Register the company (£100 online) and complete Companies House identity verification. Open a business bank account early; it is the slowest step. Run all business spending through it from day one, because settlement evidence is read from its statements.

---

### 12- and 24-Month Checkpoints

Both count from the visa grant, not the endorsement. At 12 months, progress should match the time put in; profit is not expected. At 24 months the bar rises to progress on the innovation plus commercial progress, such as engaged customers or sales. At one checkpoint the body visits your main trading premises. A co-working space or incubator counts. No premises by 24 months puts the endorsement at risk.

---

### Changing the Business Plan

Agree any change with your endorsing body before making it. A new direction must itself be innovative, viable and scalable, and a substantially different business is assessed afresh, usually for a fee. Revenue from activity the body never approved may not count at settlement.

---

### When Endorsement Is Withdrawn

Bodies withdraw when the business never started, the founder never moved or is not genuine, or checkpoint meetings are missed. The Home Office then normally cuts leave to 60 days and should let you find another body. You can still apply again later.

## Settlement (ILR) After 3 Years

After 3 years on this route, you apply for a settlement endorsement. The body must confirm that the business is active and trading and sustainable for 12 months. It must also confirm significant achievements against the endorsed plan, your day-to-day role, and at least two of these seven targets:

| Criterion | Realistic? |
|---|---|
| £50,000 invested and actively spent on the business | Yes; the most common choice |
| Customers at least doubled in 3 years, and above the average for similar UK businesses | Hard to prove; no reliable benchmark exists |
| Significant R&D plus a UK intellectual property application | Strict; a trademark does not count |
| £1 million annual gross revenue | Fast growers only |
| £500,000 revenue, £100,000 of it from exports | Export-led businesses only |
| 10 full-time jobs for settled workers | Possible, at volume |
| 5 full-time jobs for settled workers, each with a mean salary of at least £25,000 | Yes; usually paired with the £50,000 |

The pair most founders aim for is the £50,000 investment plus the five jobs. Jobs must last 12 months at an average of 30 hours a week, held by settled workers: British or Irish citizens, people with EU settled or pre-settled status, other ILR holders and UK Ancestry visa holders, but not Graduate, Student or Skilled Worker visa holders. Your own salary does not count. Co-founders cannot share a target: if both rely on the five-jobs target, the business needs ten jobs.

---

### The Sustainability Test

The rule asks whether the business is "sustainable for at least the following 12 months, based on its assets and expected income, weighed against its current and planned expenses." In practice, bodies look for revenue covering costs, including a market-rate salary for the founder role. Existing business funds can count as assets, but money injected only to reach settlement does not, and a business with no revenue at 3 years is very hard to defend.

---

### The Extension Fallback

If progress is significant but short of the targets, the body can offer a same-business endorsement instead. Extensions can repeat, and you can apply for ILR whenever ready. Settling at exactly 3 years is not expected; most founders move on to at least one extension. Before paying, check your own eligibility: no more than 180 days outside the UK in any 12 months, and the Life in the UK test if you are 18 to 64. ILR decisions can take up to 6 months.

## Switching From Another Visa

- **Graduate visa:** you can switch inside the UK. Graduate visas applied for from 1 January 2027 last 18 months, not 2 years (PhD graduates keep 3 years), so start the plan early.
- **Student visa:** you can switch inside the UK once you complete your course, or during a PhD after at least 24 months of study. Since 25 November 2025 (HC 1333), you may be self-employed on the business while an endorsed application made on Student leave is pending.
- **Skilled Worker:** you can switch inside the UK.
- **Visitor:** you cannot switch; apply from outside the UK.

Time on any other visa does not count toward the 3 years. The clock starts when the Innovator Founder visa is granted.

## Family

Your partner and children under 18 can apply as your dependants. Each pays the visa fee and the Immigration Health Surcharge (£1,035 a year for adults, £776 for children). Unless you have all been in the UK for 12 months, they also need maintenance funds held for 28 days: £285 for a partner, £315 for the first child and £200 for each further child.

A partner can work in almost any job (not as a professional sportsperson). Children can settle when you do. A partner needs 5 continuous years in the UK as your partner before settling, so a partner who arrives with you settles about 2 years after you. Both must meet English and Life in the UK requirements.

## Risks and Rule Changes

- **Earned settlement.** A consultation (Nov 2025 to Feb 2026) proposes raising the standard wait for settlement from 5 to 10 years. Innovator Founders would keep an accelerated 3-year path, but the paper also proposes earnings above £12,570 a year for 3 to 5 years and asks whether the changes should apply to people already on a route. As of Oct 2026, no government response has been published.
- **B2 English for family settlement.** From 26 March 2027 (HC 1691), partners and children need B2 speaking and listening to settle, up from B1. Founders themselves proved B2 on entry and face no new test.
- **Endorsing-body contract.** The Home Office contract for endorsing-body services runs to 23 October 2028, with an option to extend.
- **Route review.** The Migration Advisory Committee is reviewing talent routes, including the endorsing-body model, with a report due in winter 2026.
- **Falling grants.** Grants to main applicants (inside and outside the UK) fell from 880 in 2023 to 269 in the first half of 2026, a pace of about 540 a year, while the in-country refusal rate rose.

## FAQ

### Can I Work for Another Company on the Innovator Founder Visa?

The rules allow a skilled job (RQF level 3 or above) as long as the business stays your main activity. Endorsing bodies differ in how much outside work they accept, so check yours before you apply.

### Do I Need £50,000 to Get Endorsed?

No. The fixed £50,000 was removed in 2023. You need whatever your forecast shows the business needs. £50,000 survives only as one settlement target.

### Can I Apply Before Starting a Company or Trading?

Yes. Pre-trading and idea-stage businesses can be endorsed. An existing business you founded also qualifies; joining someone else's does not.

### How Long Does Innovator Founder Endorsement Take?

About 4 weeks at Innovator International and 21 working days at UK Endorsing Services (as of Oct 2026). Envestors offers no expedited service.

### Can Two Co-Founders Apply Together?

Yes, each with a separate application, separate fees and the same plan. Each needs their own two settlement targets, and the business must support both.

### What Happens If My Endorsement Is Refused?

Request a second assessment if the assessor missed a fact; otherwise fix the proposal and reapply, at any body. Repeated refusals usually point at the idea rather than the paperwork.

### Does Time on a Graduate Visa Count Toward ILR?

No. Only time on the Innovator Founder visa counts toward its 3-year settlement period.
