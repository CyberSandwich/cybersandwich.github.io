# A Guide to the Innovator Founder Visa

How the UK Innovator Founder visa works, from endorsement to settlement after 3 years.

## Summary

| | |
|---|---|
| **What it is** | A UK visa for founders whose business an approved endorsing body has judged innovative, viable and scalable |
| **Length** | 3 years, extendable for the same business |
| **Path to ILR** | Indefinite Leave to Remain after 3 years if the business meets two of seven targets |
| **Total cost** | About £10,700 to £11,700 in fees for one person over 3 years (as of Oct 2026), plus the business's own [capital](#settlement-ilr-after-3-years) |
| **Endorsement odds** | About 1 in 3 endorsed (Envestors published 34.4% for Apr 2023 to Mar 2026) |
| **Endorsement time** | About 4 to 6 weeks |
| **Visa decision** | 3 to 8 weeks on paper, often 3 to 6 months in practice |

## How It Works

1. **Build the case.** A business plan, evidence of demand and the money to launch. No company or trading history is needed.
2. **Get endorsed.** An endorsing body assesses the plan and interviews you, then issues an endorsement letter.
3. **Apply for the visa.** Use the letter within 3 months, from inside or outside the UK.
4. **Run the business.** The body reviews progress at 12 and 24 months and visits your premises once.
5. **Settle.** After 3 years, meet two of seven growth targets to get a settlement endorsement and apply for Indefinite Leave to Remain, or extend on the same business.

## Requirements

The business must pass three tests:

- **Innovative:** it offers something customers would switch to, and an ordinary competitor in the sector could not copy it quickly. Adding an app to a taxi or cleaning company is the Home Office's own example of what fails.
- **Viable:** the plan is realistic for your skills and your money, and there is evidence of real demand.
- **Scalable:** there is a structured plan to grow nationally and internationally and to create skilled jobs. A business that stays in one city, or depends on the founder's own hours, is not scalable. The innovation and its control must sit in the UK.

### Baseline Rules

- [ ] Aged 18 or over
- [ ] English at level B2 (a UK degree usually satisfies it)
- [ ] Personal maintenance funds of £1,270 held for 28 days, unless you have been in the UK for 12 months
- [ ] A business you founded or are founding; joining someone else's trading business does not qualify
- [ ] You run it day to day; a skilled side job (RQF level 3 or above) is allowed if the business stays your main work
- [ ] A sector your endorsing body assesses (crypto, adult content, politically or religiously driven projects and simple listing or comparison sites are commonly refused)

## Endorsing Bodies

Only four organizations can issue new endorsements (as of Oct 2026). The three private bodies each charge £1,000 per assessment, plus VAT for UK residents.

| Body | Best For | Turnaround |
|---|---|---|
| **Innovator International** | Most sectors | About 4 weeks, usually up to 40 days |
| **Envestors** | Investment-ready founders with long runway | About 4 to 6 weeks |
| **UK Endorsing Services** | R&D-led and deep-tech businesses | 21 working days |
| **Global Entrepreneurs Programme** | Established overseas companies, by invitation only | Not published |

Another 63 legacy bodies (as of Aug 2026) serve only founders they endorsed before 13 April 2023. If a body loses its status, the Home Office normally cuts its founders' leave to 90 days so they can find another body.

---

- **Innovator International**
  - Publishes its assessment questions; answer each one inside your plan
  - One free second assessment after a refusal
- **Envestors**
  - Its own online business plan form
  - Wants capital planned for the first 3 years, with the first 12 months' funding verified and held for 3 months
- **UK Endorsing Services**
  - A point-scored review of the plan, then an interview
- **Global Entrepreneurs Programme**
  - Applicant must be based outside the UK, with revenue from about £250,000 (depending on sector) and a completed equity raise

> [!tip] Why the Choice Matters
> The bodies read one rulebook differently on side jobs, outsourcing, combining existing products and funding evidence. A refusal by one does not bind another, but each attempt costs a fee and months.

## Getting Endorsed

Assessors usually read your CV first, then the plan, market evidence and finances. They will not research claims for you, so an unevidenced claim counts as missing.

### The Business Plan

Aim for about 30 pages plus appendices. There is no official template, and assessors distrust plans that look mass-produced, so write it yourself and use AI for wording at most. Keep desk research short and the sales plan specific, and show the product with screenshots or a demo.

Write forecasts you can beat: cost every item, get supplier quotes for anything costly, model three scenarios, and check annual sales as a daily figure. Forecasts become the benchmark at every checkpoint and at settlement, so state any pre-revenue or loss-making years up front.

### Evidence of Demand

Desk research alone is weak. Instead:

1. Interview about ten likely customers about their problem.
2. Test a priced solution with them.
3. Collect letters of intent from people who hold the budget.

Five to ten letters from company email addresses beat a survey of thousands. Name contacts in the appendix: assessors may phone them.

### Funding

There is no fixed minimum. You need enough to cover the deepest dip in your cash-flow forecast, plus contingency, and at least 12 months of runway. The money must be in cash and held for some months (bodies ask for 3 to 6 months of statements). Property, a car for sale and future salary do not count. Third-party money needs the funder's bank evidence and a signed letter of terms.

> [!tip] Only Money You Hold Counts
> A pending grant, an investor "in talks" or a raise planned after arrival does not count. Build the main forecast on money you hold and show any raise as a second scenario.

---

### Founder Skills

You need sector or technical skill, plus the business skill to sell and run the company. Gaps are fine if named and funded. A founder with only a small minority stake can expect questions about control.

> [!warning] Own the Technology
> You may subcontract the coding, but you must design the innovation yourself and have someone technical own the build. The Home Office's guidance gives, as an example to refuse, a sole founder of an AI platform with no technical skill to own it. Consultancies offering to "build it for you" create exactly this risk.

### Interview and Due Diligence

Bodies interview at different points. Innovator International may raise questions on borderline plans first and interviews only plans that pass; Envestors interviews in every assessment. The interview checks you wrote the plan and can explain your numbers. Due diligence covers identity, sanctions and the source of funds. Deal with the bodies directly: forged endorsement letters and firms falsely claiming to partner with a body are in circulation.

### After a Refusal

Send your strongest version first, because later applications are read alongside earlier ones. A second assessment helps only when you can point to facts the assessor missed, and the window to ask is short. A rewritten proposal is a new paid application, at any body.

## Applying and Processing Times

The endorsement letter is valid for 3 months, so do not request it too early. Expect to upload the plan and evidence again with the visa application, because the caseworker reads the file afresh.

### Switching From Another Visa

- **Graduate visa:** you can switch inside the UK. Graduate visas applied for from 1 January 2027 last 18 months, not 2 years (PhD graduates keep 3 years), so start the plan early.
- **Student visa:** you can switch inside the UK once you complete your course, or during a PhD after at least 24 months of study. Since 25 November 2025 you may work self-employed on the business while your visa application is pending.
- **Skilled Worker:** you can switch inside the UK.
- **Visitor:** you cannot switch; apply from outside the UK.

An application made before your visa expires keeps your leave going while it is decided, but if it is refused after the expiry date you cannot make a fresh application from inside the UK. Leave room for a second endorsement attempt. The 3 years start at the Innovator Founder grant; time on other visas does not count.

### Processing Times

The Home Office aims to decide in 3 weeks from outside the UK and 8 weeks from inside, with paid priority (5 working days) and super priority (next working day). Founders report 3 to 6 months in practice as of Oct 2026, priority included, and priority slots are hard to book.

> [!warning] Rising Refusals After Endorsement
> Refusals of in-UK applications by main applicants (switches and extensions on this route and the old Innovator route) rose from 3.2% in 2021 to 31.4% in Q2 2026. Caseworkers send written questions about who built the product, funding, pricing and claimed customer conversations. Keep evidence you can share for every claim in the plan.

## Costs

Fees to settlement for one applicant applying inside the UK (as of Oct 2026). Endorsing-body fees carry 20% VAT for UK residents.

| Item | Cost |
|---|---:|
| Initial endorsement | £1,000 (£1,200 with VAT) |
| Visa application, inside the UK | £1,693 |
| Immigration Health Surcharge, 3 years at £1,035 | £3,105 |
| 12- and 24-month checkpoints | £1,000 (£1,200 with VAT) |
| Settlement endorsement | £1,000 (£1,200 with VAT) |
| ILR application plus Life in the UK test | £3,276 |
| **Total** | **£11,074 (£11,674 with VAT)** |

- **From outside the UK:** the visa fee is £1,357 and the total £10,738 (£11,338 with VAT).
- **Same-business extension:** about £7,198 more if the business is not ready to settle.
- **Refused endorsement:** fees are not refunded.
- **Family:** each member adds their own fees; see [Family](#family).

## After You Arrive

### First Steps

Register the company (£100 online) and complete Companies House identity verification; you must be listed as a director, and the filings must stay up to date. Open a business bank account early; it is the slowest step. Run all business money through it from day one, because settlement evidence is read from its statements.

### 12- and 24-Month Checkpoints

Tell your endorsing body the day your visa is granted: it may not hear from the Home Office, and the checkpoints count from that date. Attend both, because an extension later requires at least two checkpoint meetings.

- **12 months:** progress should be reasonable for the time; profit is not expected.
- **24 months:** the body also expects commercial progress, such as engaged customers or sales.

At one checkpoint the body visits your main trading premises, which can be a co-working space or incubator; having none by 24 months puts the endorsement at risk.

### Changing the Business Plan

Agree any change with your endorsing body before making it. A new direction must itself be innovative, viable and scalable, and a substantially different business is assessed afresh, usually for a fee. If a forecast proves wrong, ask the body to agree a revised forecast instead of carrying a target you cannot meet. Revenue from activity the body never approved may not count at settlement.

### When Endorsement Is Withdrawn

An endorsement is withdrawn if the business never starts, you never move to the UK, you are not genuine, or you miss checkpoints. The Home Office then normally cuts your leave to 60 days and should let you find another body.

## Settlement (ILR) After 3 Years

Apply for a settlement endorsement about 3 months before your visa ends: the body takes 4 to 6 weeks, and your ILR application must be in before your leave expires. The body must confirm that the business is active, trading and sustainable for the next 12 months, has significant achievements against the endorsed plan, is run by you day to day, and meets at least two of these seven targets:

- £50,000 invested and actively spent on the business
- Customers at least doubled in 3 years and above the average for similar UK businesses (no reliable benchmark exists)
- Significant R&D plus a UK intellectual property application (a trademark does not count)
- £1 million annual gross revenue
- £500,000 revenue, £100,000 of it from exports
- 10 full-time jobs for settled workers
- 5 full-time jobs for settled workers, each paying at least £25,000 a year

The two jobs targets count as only one of your two. Most founders aim for the £50,000 plus the five jobs, and evidence a third target where they can, in case one is disallowed.

### Meeting the Targets

- **The £50,000** must be new money you put in, not reinvested profit. Both the money in and the spending must show on the company's business account. Visa and endorsement fees and your own salary do not count.
- **The jobs** must each last 12 months at an average of 30 hours a week, so to settle at 3 years, hire by about month 22. Each role must pay at least £25,000 by itself; you cannot average across roles. Anyone counted, a co-founder included, needs a written employment contract.
- **Settled workers**, as the rules define them, are British or Irish citizens, people with EU settled or pre-settled status, other ILR holders and UK Ancestry visa holders. Graduate, Student and Skilled Worker visa holders do not count.
- **Co-founders** cannot share a target: if both rely on the five jobs, the business needs ten.
- **The cost:** five £25,000 jobs come to about £132,000 to £143,000 a year with employer National Insurance and pension (2026 to 2027 rates), on top of the £50,000.

### Before You Apply

Check your own eligibility: no more than 180 days outside the UK in any 12 months, and the Life in the UK test if you are 18 to 64. The ILR application can go in no earlier than 28 days before you complete 3 years, or it may be refused. Decisions can take up to 6 months, and travelling outside the UK, Ireland, the Channel Islands or the Isle of Man before then withdraws the application.

---

### The Sustainability Test

The rule asks whether the business is "sustainable for at least the following 12 months, based on its assets and expected income, weighed against its current and planned expenses." In practice, bodies look for sales that cover costs and a market-rate salary for the founder role. Savings that cover a trading loss will not normally pass, though cash already in the business counts as an asset. Only revenue from the endorsed innovation, earned by the endorsed company, counts: bodies may exclude income from doing the work by hand, from consultancy that has become the main business, or paid into another company.

### The Extension Fallback

If progress is significant but short of the targets, the body can offer a same-business endorsement instead, on the same 3-month timing. Extensions can repeat, and you can apply for ILR once the targets are met; most founders need at least one.

## Family

Your partner and children under 18 can apply as your dependants. Each pays the visa fee and the Immigration Health Surcharge (£1,035 a year for adults, £776 for children). Unless you have all been in the UK for 12 months, they also need maintenance funds held for 28 days: £285 for a partner, £315 for the first child and £200 for each further child.

- **Partner:** married, in a civil partnership, or together for at least 2 years (living together or able to show ongoing commitment). A partner can work in almost any job and needs 5 continuous years in the UK as your partner before settling, so one who arrives with you settles about 2 years after you. Your partner must pass the Life in the UK test.
- **Children:** can settle when you do.

From 26 March 2027, partners and children need B2 English speaking and listening to settle, up from B1. Founders are unaffected.

## Risks and Rule Changes

- **Earned settlement.** A consultation (Nov 2025 to Feb 2026) proposes raising the standard wait for settlement from 5 to 10 years. Innovator Founders would keep an accelerated 3-year path, but the paper also proposes requiring earnings above £12,570 a year for 3 to 5 years before settling, and asks whether the changes should apply to people already on a route. As of Oct 2026, no government response has been published.
- **Endorsing bodies.** The Home Office contract for endorsing-body services runs to 23 October 2028, and the Migration Advisory Committee is reviewing the endorsing-body model, with a report due in winter 2026.

## FAQ

### Can I Work for Another Company on the Innovator Founder Visa?

Yes, in a skilled job (RQF level 3 or above) if the business stays your main activity, though bodies differ in how much outside work they accept.

### Do I Need £50,000 to Get Endorsed?

No. The fixed £50,000 was removed in 2023; it survives only as one settlement target.

### Can Two Co-Founders Apply Together?

Yes, each with a separate application and fees on the same plan, and each founder needs their own two settlement targets.
